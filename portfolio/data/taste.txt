# taste
#
# The other half of a portfolio. The rest of this app argues that I can build
# things; this argues about what I think is worth building things for.
#
# Same grammar as everywhere else: two spaces starts a key, four or more
# continues the previous value.
#
#   emblem  which drawing from scripts/emblems.py stands for this entry.
#           Original artwork, not a still — partly because a 40x20 dither of a
#           copyrighted frame is a bad idea, and partly because an object a
#           character carries says more about them than their face does.

open  There is a version of a portfolio that is only a list of things I can do.
      This is the other half. These are the characters, films and shows I keep
      returning to, and what I think they have in common — which turned out to
      be a more honest description of me than any list of languages.

# ── the people ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

figure snufkin
  name    Snufkin
  from    Moominvalley · Tove Jansson
  emblem  hat
  line    freedom that is not a refusal of people
  body    He wanders, plays the harmonica, and leaves every spring without
          apologising for it. What makes him more than an aesthetic is that he
          cares about Moomintroll enormously and still goes — he has worked out
          that loving someone and needing to be near them are different things.
          Very few characters are allowed to be that independent and that warm
          at the same time.

figure little-prince
  name    The Little Prince
  from    Antoine de Saint-Exupéry
  emblem  planet
  line    the rose is ordinary; it is yours that matters
  body    A book about how attention creates value. Five thousand roses in a
          garden and none of them are his, because he did not water any of
          them. It is the clearest thing I have read about why the time you
          put into something is not the price of the thing but the whole
          substance of it.

figure iroh
  name    Uncle Iroh
  from    Avatar: The Last Airbender
  emblem  teacup
  line    wisdom that has already paid for itself
  body    He is patient because he has lost a son, not because patience comes
          easily to him. That is the distinction that makes him work. His
          calm is not temperament, it is a position he arrived at, and the tea
          is not a quirk — it is the practice of noticing small good things on
          purpose.

figure ted
  name    Ted Lasso
  from    Ted Lasso
  emblem  sign
  line    kindness as a discipline, not a temperament
  body    The show's trick is that Ted is not fine. He has panic attacks, a
          marriage ending, and no idea what offside is. The kindness costs him
          something every time, which is the only reason it is interesting.
          Radical decency practised by someone who is doing badly is a much
          harder argument to dismiss.

figure miles
  name    Miles Morales
  from    Into the Spider-Verse
  emblem  web
  line    you take the leap before you are ready
  body    Every other version of the story is about being chosen. This one is
          about deciding, which is harder and truer. The whole film is built
          around the moment you stop waiting to feel qualified — and it is
          honest that the leap comes before the confidence, not after it.

figure bourdain
  name    Anthony Bourdain
  from    Parts Unknown · Kitchen Confidential
  emblem  bowl
  line    eat with people and let them talk
  body    He is the bridge between an interest in places and an interest in
          people. Not travel as a list of ten attractions — travel as sitting
          down with someone, eating what they eat, and letting the conversation
          go wherever it goes: history, politics, loneliness, whether they are
          happy. Food was only ever the excuse.

# ── the work ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

work ikiru
  name    Ikiru
  from    Kurosawa · 1952
  emblem  swing
  line    what makes a life, as opposed to a career
  body    A bureaucrat finds out he is dying and spends what is left getting a
          playground built. The famous shot is him on the swing in the snow,
          and the reason it lands is that the film has already shown you thirty
          years of him not doing anything. It asks the question directly:
          what did you actually make.

work silent-voice
  name    A Silent Voice
  from    Naoko Yamada · 2016
  emblem  notebook
  line    whether someone who did harm can become good
  body    It refuses the easy version where the bully was secretly kind. He was
          not. The film is about guilt that does not resolve, isolation that is
          partly deserved, and the enormous difficulty of being forgiven — and
          then of accepting it, which turns out to be the harder half.

work spiderverse
  name    Into the Spider-Verse
  from    2018
  emblem  glitch
  line    identity, expectation, and taking the leap
  body    On the surface a superhero film. Underneath, a film about being the
          wrong version of the thing you are supposed to be, and finding out
          that is the point. Also the most formally inventive animation in
          twenty years, which is not nothing.

work the-bear
  name    The Bear
  from    2022 —
  emblem  knife
  line    craft as both the wound and the cure
  body    Obsession with getting something right, in a kitchen full of grief
          and people who cannot talk to each other. It is the most accurate
          thing on television about caring enormously about work and being bad
          at the human parts, and it does not pretend those are unrelated.

work haikyuu
  name    Haikyuu!!
  from    Haruichi Furudate
  emblem  ball
  line    the joy is in getting better, not in winning
  body    A sports story where the rival is never the villain — the best moments
          are two people making each other better across a net. Losing is
          allowed to be sad and also not the end of anything. Improvement is
          treated as its own reward, which is rarer than it sounds.

work one-piece
  name    One Piece
  from    Eiichiro Oda
  emblem  strawhat
  line    absurd, enormous, and completely sincere
  body    A thousand episodes about refusing to let the world make you smaller.
          Chosen family, ridiculous jokes, and a dream stated so plainly that
          irony cannot get a grip on it. It is the least embarrassed piece of
          art I love.

# ── what it adds up to ────────────────────────────────────────────────────

thread freedom
  name  Freedom
  body  Snufkin leaving in spring, Luffy sailing wherever he likes, Miles
        deciding what the mask means, Bourdain going somewhere without
        sanitising it. Not freedom *from* people — freedom that still has
        people in it.

thread connection
  name  Connection
  body  In all of these, the relationships are not the subplot. They are the
        plot. A Silent Voice, Ted Lasso, One Piece, The Bear, the Little
        Prince and his rose — take the relationships out and there is nothing
        left standing.

thread kindness
  name  Kindness with weight
  body  Not nice characters. Iroh has buried a son, Ted is coming apart, Miles
        pays for every right decision. Their kindness counts precisely because
        it is chosen against something, which is the difference between a
        virtue and a personality trait.

thread purpose
  name  Purpose
  body  Ikiru asks it outright and the rest keep circling it: not what makes
        someone successful, but what makes a life worth having had. Usually
        the answer is something small, built for other people, finished.

thread craft
  name  Craft
  body  The Bear and Haikyuu are both about becoming excellent, and neither
        thinks excellence is about status. The process is what changes the
        person. That is also, more or less, why this whole thing is written in
        Rust instead of being a web page.

thread earnestness
  name  Earnestness
  body  The common thread, and maybe the whole list. Every one of these is
        willing to be sincere without hiding behind irony first. They are
        funny, absurd and often sad, and they still say plainly that
        friendship and dreams and food and small human moments matter.

close Snufkin's freedom, the Prince's attention, Iroh's patience, Ted's
      decency, Miles's nerve, Bourdain's curiosity. That is a better
      description of what I am aiming at than "likes Linux and maps" — though
      I do like Linux and maps.
