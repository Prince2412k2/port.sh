# taste
#
# The through-line is freedom and kindness: people who are free of other
# people, or of themselves, and are kind with it. Say what each thing is and
# where the freedom is in it. No verdicts, no reviews.
#
#   emblem  which drawing from scripts/emblems.py stands for this entry.
#           Drawn here rather than fetched: a low-resolution crop of somebody
#           else's frame is a poor thing to put on a public server, and what a
#           character carries reads better at this size than their face.

open  Almost everything here is about someone getting free of other people or
      of themselves, and being kind about it.

figure snufkin
  name    Snufkin
  from    Moominvalley
  emblem  hat
  line    Free of everybody and still fond of them. He goes south every autumn
          and nobody has to be hurt by it, which is the harder half.

figure bourdain
  name    Anthony Bourdain
  from    Parts Unknown
  emblem  bowl
  line    Snufkin with a camera crew. Goes anywhere, owes nobody an itinerary,
          and the reason for arriving is to sit down and let someone talk.

figure iroh
  name    Uncle Iroh
  from    Avatar: The Last Airbender
  emblem  teacup
  line    Spends the whole show getting Zuko free of an expectation that had
          curdled into pride. Patient about it because he has already lost more.

figure ted
  name    Ted Lasso
  from    Ted Lasso
  emblem  sign
  line    Free of judgement, which is a different thing from being nice. He
          decides people are worth it before they have shown him anything.

figure miles
  name    Miles Morales
  from    Spider-Verse
  emblem  web
  line    In the first film he jumps before he is ready. In the second he is
          told the story is fixed and that people have to be lost, and refuses.

figure little-prince
  name    The Little Prince
  from    Saint-Exupéry
  emblem  planet
  line    Free of the way grown-ups price things. The rose matters because he
          watered it, not because there is anything rare about a rose.

work ikiru
  name    Ikiru
  from    Kurosawa, 1952
  emblem  swing
  line    A clerk finds out he is dying and it frees him. Thirty years of
          shuffling paper, then he gets a playground built and sits in it.

work one-piece
  name    One Piece
  from    Oda
  emblem  strawhat
  line    The most direct version. A boy wants to be free, the world is
          arranged to stop him, and a thousand episodes later nobody has quit.

work the-bear
  name    The Bear
  from    2022
  emblem  knife
  line    A kitchen full of grief trying to get one plate right. Everyone in it
          is working their way out from under something.

work silent-voice
  name    A Silent Voice
  from    Yamada, 2016
  emblem  notebook
  line    Whether you get to be free of something you did. He is not let off,
          and still has to find a way to live in the same town.

close People and things that remind me of kindness and freedom. That is the
      whole list.
