# taste
#
# The through-line is freedom and kindness: people who are free of other
# people, or of themselves, and are kind with it.
#
# Nothing here is in my words. Each entry is the thing itself saying it, and
# the page is a wall of quotations rather than a set of reviews -- a one-line
# verdict on somebody's life's work reads as cheap no matter how it is phrased,
# and these are better lines than mine anyway.
#
#   quote   what they said. Theirs, not mine. Keep it short enough to sit
#           beside the drawing; the shelf turns back into an essay past two
#           lines.
#   emblem  which drawing from scripts/emblems.py stands for this entry.
#   wall    which scene from src/walls.rs hangs behind it in the room. Every
#           work gets its own: the room is about the thing, not a texture. The
#           names are rain, flights, ripples, pitch, web, stars, sea and
#           contours -- and `contours` is the plain wall, which is also what an
#           entry gets if it names a scene that does not exist.

open  Almost everything here is about someone getting free of other people or
      of themselves, and being kind about it.
